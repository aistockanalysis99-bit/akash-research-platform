- Stock A too low vs B: BUY A + SHORT B

9. Exact Profit Target & Stop Loss:

10. Balanced Beta Position Sizing:
Share quantities are weighted by the hedge ratio so overall market ups and downs don't matter — we only make money on the gap closing.
